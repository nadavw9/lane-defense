-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY TWO PRIOR FIXES MISSED IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both earlier fixes are live in production — verified against the deployed bundle's
ancestry, not git log (e7b4720 and c35d40f are both ancestors of the deployed
091d9e9). They were not the wrong fixes; they were fixes to the wrong path. Both
targeted the refill DROP. The actual trigger is the MERGE.

The merge's 'travel' phase spent 150ms interpolating each outer bomb's world
position across the queue to the destination slot. Sockets are Pixi circles at
fixed screen positions; balls are Three meshes. So a ball in flight is a ball drawn
outside every socket — measured on the live build at up to 2.00 SLOT PITCHES, two
entire sockets, from its own.

Measured with a per-frame trace (scripts/_ball-timeseries.mjs) rather than a
before/after snapshot, which is what finally separated the merge from the refill.

ONE CORRECTION TO WHAT I TOLD YOU EARLIER: I first reported ~370ms of visible
displacement. That was wrong — it counted spent travellers parked at the
destination with scale 0, which are invisible. The genuinely visible window is
~120ms. The magnitude, not the duration, is what makes it screenshot-able: a ball
two full sockets away from where it belongs is obvious in any single frame.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIX IS STRUCTURAL, NOT A RE-TUNE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per your decision: a ball is now either seated in a socket or not drawn at all.
The outers shrink out where they already sit; the merged bomb pops in AT the
destination. Nothing travels between slots.

This kills the class, not just this instance — no easing curve makes a path
between two sockets correct, because every point along it is outside both.
Guarded by tests/merge-animation-geometry.test.js (4 tests): the travel, highlight
and pop phases may not translate a slot; exactly ONE slot translation may exist in
GameApp (the refill drop, separately bounded); and the cross-slot interpolation
caches must stay deleted rather than merely unused.
