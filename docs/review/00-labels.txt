WHAT CHANGED — two separate faults, one symptom:

1. Travel was measured from a HARDCODED world Z (DROP_START_Z = -1.0), so distance
   was `target.z - (-1.0)` — different for every row AND every band:
       row 0 travelled 1.4 slot pitches, row 2 travelled 3.4 — in the SAME 150ms
       and the bottom-chrome reclaim stretched the far rows another 40%
   Now each bomb starts a fixed 1.5 SLOT PITCHES above its own slot, so travel is
   identical for every row, at every lane count and band.

2. The drop reused the merge pop's easeOutBack (~10.2% overshoot). Across the old
   long travel that threw the ball ~5.7px past its slot — far enough to clear the
   socket ring, which is exactly the "out of the slot" read. The drop now uses a
   softer curve (~5.4%, ~3px); the merge POP is unchanged, since that is a scale
   bounce, not a position move.

Guarded by tests/drop-animation-geometry.test.js (6 tests): travel identical
across rows, identical in pitch units across bands, bounded to <=2 pitches, and
the peak overshoot proven to stay INSIDE the socket ring at every lane count.
