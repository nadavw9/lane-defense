transition-1-buggy-L13toL5.png   THE REPORTED BUG. Load L13 (4-lane, band 540) then
                                 L5 (3-lane, band 600). Empty socket rings sit at their
                                 correct band-600 positions while the BALLS stay at
                                 band-540 spacing (pitch 2.296 vs correct 1.835). Error

                                 bottom row clearing the bench with visible margin.
                                 pitch 1.8348 = correct for band 600.

Cause: Shooter3D.update() lazily latched g._baseZ once and then pinned position.z to
it every frame. setLaneCount() assigns the new band's slotZ, and the next update tick
silently undid it. Fix: read slotZ(si) live; the cache is gone.
