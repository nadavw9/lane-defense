Traffic Bomb — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Traffic Bomb is a puzzle game made by an independent developer, Nadav W. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: there are no accounts, we never ask for your name, email, contacts or precise location, and your game progress stays on your phone.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters) and settings (sound, haptics, colour-blind mode) are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To balance difficulty, the game sends anonymous statistics to Google Firebase: a random ID created on your device, which level was played, whether it was won or lost, how long it took, how many moves and boosters were used, and your screen size. This ID is not linked to your name, email, advertising ID or any account, and we cannot identify you from it.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional ads you choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions. How Google uses this is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Children

Traffic Bomb is rated for Everyone. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase and Google AdMob) for the purposes described.

Deleting your data

Clearing the game's data or uninstalling removes everything stored on your device and starts a new random ID. To ask for anonymous statistics to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
