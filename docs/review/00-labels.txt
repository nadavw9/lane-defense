===============================================================================
05-alignment-BEFORE-L13toL5.png   the reported bug
06-alignment-AFTER-L13toL5.png    fixed

HOW THESE WERE CAPTURED — this matters: load L13 (4-lane, band 540) FIRST, then
L5 (3-lane, band 600). A fresh L5 load does NOT reproduce the bug, because the
stale value latches from the FIRST level of the session. A rest screenshot of L5
alone would have shown a perfectly clean queue.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN 05 (BEFORE) — this is your "bombs stuck out of their slots":
  - Empty socket RINGS are visible sitting at their correct positions, with the
    BALLS floating between them rather than inside them.
  - The misalignment GROWS down the column: row 0 nearly right, row 2 badly off.
  - The bottom row's balls are cut off by the bench strip.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN 06 (AFTER):
  - Every ball centred in its socket, all three rows, both compared side by side.
  - Bottom row clears the bench strip with visible margin.

CAUSE: Shooter3D.update() latched the slot Z once and then pinned position to it
every frame. setLaneCount() assigned the new band's slot Z and the next update
tick silently undid it — so the balls used the pitch of the session's FIRST level
forever, while the Pixi sockets recomputed live. Fix: read slotZ() live, no cache.

>>> STILL NEEDS YOUR DEVICE: please confirm alignment holds when you actually
    cross a band boundary in play (e.g. finish L8 -> L9, or L13 -> L5 via level
    select), not just on a fresh level load.

PAIR C — THE "BOMBS OUT OF THEIR SLOTS" YOU REPORTED AT L8  (DIAGNOSIS ONLY,
         nothing changed — the fix is a feel decision and it is yours)
===============================================================================
07-drop-AT-REST.png          L8 at rest: every ball centred in its socket.
08-drop-MID-ANIMATION.png    L8 caught DURING the post-fire refill: this is your
                             screenshot. Bottom-row balls sit low/overshot with
                             their socket rings visibly EMPTY above them.

WHAT IT IS: the refill DROP-IN animation, not a position bug. Refilling slots are
_animLock'd, and Shooter3D.update() skips locked slots entirely, so the ball sits
wherever the tween puts it. Balls fly in from a HARDCODED world Z (DROP_START_Z
= -1.0) over 150ms with a 50ms per-row stagger, easing with easeOutBack — which
deliberately OVERSHOOTS past the slot before settling.

MEASURED (L8, column 0, one shot): lock set t=553ms, released t=1284ms — 731ms
locked, with the visible excursion in the last ~161ms, peaking 3.43 world units
off rest (~1.9 slot pitches). The "snap-back a second later" is the sequence
ending: _finish() -> clearBombAnimLocks().

WHY IT GOT WORSE: DROP_START_Z is a fixed world Z that does NOT track the queue
geometry. The bottom-chrome reclaim moved slot 2 from z~3.44 to z~5.21, so the
drop now travels ~6.2 world units instead of ~4.4 (+40%) in the SAME 150ms —
faster streak, bigger overshoot, far more noticeable. Same stale-constant class
as the _baseZ latch fixed in e7b4720.

>>> YOUR CALL: this is animation feel, so nothing was changed. Options are to
    derive DROP_START_Z from the queue (constant travel distance at any band),
    lengthen the 150ms, or reduce the easeOutBack overshoot.
